THE HISTORICAL TIMELINE

Explore the ages of Iran.

Move through time and discover the major periods that shaped the Iranian world.

01
c. 3200–539 BCE

Elamite Civilization

One of the earliest civilizations of the Iranian plateau, Elam developed important urban centers and administrative traditions. Its capital, Susa, became a major center of regional culture and political power.

EARLY CIVILIZATION
02
c. 7th–6th century BCE

Median Kingdom

The Medes established a major political power in western Iran. Their rise helped prepare the ground for the emergence of the Achaemenid Empire.

EARLY IRANIAN KINGDOM
03
550–330 BCE

Achaemenid Empire

Founded by Cyrus the Great, the Achaemenid Empire became one of the largest empires of the ancient world. Its administration, Royal Road, monumental architecture, and diverse imperial society left a lasting legacy.

ANCIENT EMPIRE
04
247 BCE–224 CE

Parthian Empire

The Parthians built a powerful empire between the eastern world and the Mediterranean. Their cavalry, diplomacy, and control of important trade routes helped them resist Roman expansion for centuries.

TRADE & MILITARY POWER
05
224–651 CE

Sasanian Empire

The Sasanians developed a centralized state known for monumental architecture, scholarship, trade, and rivalry with Rome and Byzantium. Their cultural and political traditions influenced later Iranian history.

LATE ANTIQUITY
06
821–873 CE

Tahirid Dynasty

Based in Khorasan, the Tahirids governed an important regional power under the Abbasid caliphate and contributed to the development of local Iranian rule.

REGIONAL DYNASTY
07
861–1003 CE

Saffarid Dynasty

Emerging in Sistan, the Saffarids expanded their power across parts of eastern Iran. Their rise formed an important chapter in the growth of regional Iranian dynasties.

EASTERN IRAN
08
819–999 CE

Samanid Empire

The Samanids supported Persian literature, scholarship, and urban culture. Their era played a major role in the revival and development of New Persian literary traditions.

CULTURE & LITERATURE
09
977–1186 CE

Ghaznavid Dynasty

The Ghaznavids ruled a large territory extending into parts of Iran and South Asia. Their court supported Persian poetry, scholarship, and monumental architecture.

PERSIANATE COURT
10
1037–1194 CE

Seljuk Empire

The Seljuks shaped a vast political and cultural world in which Persian administration, architecture, education, and literature flourished.

ART & ARCHITECTURE
11
1501–1736 CE

Safavid Empire

The Safavids unified much of Iran under a centralized state and established Twelver Shiism as the state religion. Isfahan became a renowned center of art, architecture, trade, and urban design.

A NEW IRANIAN STATE
12
1736–1796 CE

Afsharid Dynasty

Founded by Nader Shah, the Afsharids restored a powerful military state and expanded Iranian influence into neighboring regions during the eighteenth century.

MILITARY POWER
13
1751–1794 CE

Zand Dynasty

Under Karim Khan Zand, Shiraz became an important center of architecture, commerce, and cultural life. The period is remembered for its distinctive buildings and urban projects.

SHIRAZ & CULTURE
14
1789/1796–1925 CE

Qajar Dynasty

The Qajar period witnessed major political changes, territorial losses, constitutional movements, and the gradual introduction of modern institutions and education.

CONSTITUTIONAL CHANGE
15
1925–1979 CE

Pahlavi Dynasty

Iran underwent extensive changes in infrastructure, education, industry, government, and urban life. These transformations shaped many aspects of modern Iranian society.

MODERN TRANSFORMATION
16
1979–PRESENT

Contemporary Iran

The 1979 Revolution established the Islamic Republic. Contemporary Iran continues to evolve through its diverse society, cultural heritage, scientific achievements, and changing regional and international relationships.
